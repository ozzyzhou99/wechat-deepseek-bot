# Group Participant Notice Template / 群成员告知模板

> Adapt this template to your actual processing activities and obtain legal review where appropriate.

## 中文

本群拟使用一个 AI 助手。启用前，请向成员说明并取得必要授权：

- 处理者和联系方式：`[名称/联系方式]`
- 目的：`[例如：仅回应主动提问]`
- 数据：`[例如：当前消息文本、显示昵称、时间；如不保存历史请明确说明]`
- 是否发送给外部 LLM：`[服务商、目的、数据范围]`
- 保存期限与存储位置：`[期限/位置/保护措施]`
- 成员权利与退出/删除方式：`[操作方式]`

成员在未同意前不应被纳入归档或外部 LLM 处理。涉及敏感个人信息、未成年人信息、跨境传输、商业运营或大规模处理时，请先取得专业法律意见。

## English

Before enabling an AI assistant in a group, clearly state the controller and contact details, purpose, data categories, external-LLM transfer, retention/storage safeguards, and the access, withdrawal, and deletion route. Do not archive or send a participant's content to an external LLM before the required notice and lawful basis are in place. Obtain professional advice for sensitive data, minors, cross-border transfers, commercial use, or large-scale processing.
